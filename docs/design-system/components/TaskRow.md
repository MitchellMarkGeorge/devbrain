# TaskRow

One task in a strokeless list: no line beneath, a `row-height` (48px) row with 8px corners that takes the `row-selected` tint on hover and when selected.

- **Leading control.** The status icon is a button: click it (or press `S` on a focused row) for the `StatusMenu`, then `1`, `2` or `3`. The selection checkbox sits in a strip to its left and shows on hover, on keyboard focus, and on every row while any row is selected (`X` toggles the focused row).
- **Title first.** The name in 14px `foreground-strong`, then pills:
  - a synced task's `SourcePill`: the provider's logo and the key ("[Linear] ENG-124"), so where the task comes from and its key read as one unit;
  - on a subtask, its parent (`EntityPill type="parent"`: corner arrow, status icon, parent title), left off in a task page's own Subtasks list, where the parent is the page;
  - on a parent task, the subtask count in a pill (`ProgressRing` and "3/4").
- **When the row runs out of room,** the pills on each side stack like a deck (the last in full, the ones behind showing a 26px edge with their icon) and spread out on hover or focus, drawn over the end of the title on the row's own ground (never past the start of the title) so nothing in the row moves; the row keeps its height. Stacking and spreading animate in `duration-normal`: as the row narrows or widens, or a stack is pointed at, the pills behind grow or shrink between full width and their edge and their labels fade; a spread stack's ground fades in first (`duration-fast`). A row draws its first level still. The title shortens only after that. The row measures itself and stacks as far as it needs to (`stack="auto"`, the default); `stack="right"` stacks the pills after the title, and `stack="both"` also stacks the key with the subtask or parent pill; a side with one pill never stacks. In Figma this is TaskRow's `pills` variant (`spread`, `stacked right`, `stacked both`).
- **Right side.** `EntityPill` chips (the linked event or note, then the project with its folder icon in `projectColor`, so the project is the pill a stack shows in full), `PriorityIcon`, and the date (`date-today`, `date-overdue` or `subtle-foreground`, 44px right-aligned).
- **Anatomy.** The 24px status slot starts 36px from the row's outer edge; the checkbox sits 9px from it. 10px between the status, the title and the right side; 8px between the right side's parts.
- **States.** Rest; hover (`row-selected`, the same tint as selected); selected (`row-selected`, title in `row-selected-foreground`; neighbouring selected rows join into one block); keyboard focus (the hover tint with a 1px `ring` outline around the whole row, no halo, as Figma draws it); completed (title in `muted-foreground`, not struck through, no priority); overdue (the flag status icon and `date-overdue`); subtask (indented under its parent).
- `task`: `{ title, status, key?, source?, parent?, subtasks?, project?, projectColor?, event?, eventColor?, note?, priority, date?, dateTone?, selected? }`. `stack`: `'auto'` (the default: the row measures itself), or `'none' | 'right' | 'both'` to pin a level. Use it inside `TaskList`, which handles selection.
