# PageHeader

A page's frame and the parts it is built from, for lists, cards, the calendar, Settings and detail pages. Each page draws its own: the shell holds nothing of the page's, so a page decides whether it has a header and what is in it, and can lay itself out as it needs.

- **`Page`:** what every page in a `WorkspaceShell` renders: its `title`, its `header`, then everything else in the page's scroller, a `ScrollArea` (its scrollbar drawn over the page's edge, taking no width) whose scrolling element carries `data-slot="page-scroll"`; the app's router scrolls it to the top on a new page and restores it on Back and Forward. Inside a `WorkspaceShell`:
  - the **`title`** ("Tasks", a task's or note's title, "Settings · General") shows in the title bar's search field, which says where you are and opens search: `Page` portals it in (`useWorkspaceShell().locationContainer`). A page still loading leaves it out, and the field reads "Search or jump to…". There are no breadcrumbs.
  - the **`header`** goes at the title bar's end: `Page` portals it into the shell's slot (`useWorkspaceShell().titleBarContainer`), so it stays put, takes no room in the page's card, and is still the page's own JSX (its menus use the page's state). A page with no actions there leaves `header` out.

  The page's content starts at the card's top, and a `PageToolbar` there keeps 12px clear of its edge (`data-in-shell`). Outside a shell (stories, tests) the page draws its header at its top, and the title isn't drawn. `scrollClassName` styles the scroller.

- **`PageHeader`:** the page's actions at the right, 24px in from the side, 8px apart: a detail page's star (`PageFavoriteButton`, filled in `date-today` while on) and "…" menu (`PageMenuButton`, aligned to the right edge). In the title bar it is as tall as the bar (the shell sets `--page-header-height`), its buttons don't drag the window and the rest of it does, and on Windows it stops short of the caption buttons (both the shell's doing); outside a shell it tops the page, 48px tall. Its children, if any, go at the left (pages in a shell pass none).
- **`PageToolbar`:** the row under it: `start` (Filter) and `end` (Sort, the view switch, the quiet "New …" button), 32px controls 8px apart, 12px above the content. Toolbar buttons show their value after the label; in compact windows pass `hideValue`.
- **`PageContent`:** the page under them. `list` and `grid` fill the height under the toolbar and pass it on as a flex column, so the `TaskList`, `Board` or `VirtualCardGrid` placed in them fills it and scrolls itself while the header and toolbar stay put (keep the list a direct child, or give a wrapper `display: flex; flex-direction: column; flex: 1; min-height: 0`). `list` keeps rows 16px from the sides, `grid` cards 24px, `home` Home's 800px column (`home-max-width`), centred across the page and, while it fits, down it, and `document` centres a 720px column (`content-max-width`) 48px under the header: notes, tasks, Settings and Integrations. `home` and `document` keep their own height, and the `Page`'s scroller scrolls them whole.

**Base UI:** `Menu` for the "…" menu; the rest is plain markup.
