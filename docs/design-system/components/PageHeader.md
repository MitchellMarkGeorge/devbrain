# PageHeader

A page's frame and the parts it is built from, for lists, cards, the calendar, Settings and detail pages. Each page draws its own: the shell holds nothing of the page's, so a page decides whether it has a header and what is in it, and can lay itself out as it needs.

- **`Page`:** what every page in a `WorkspaceShell` renders: its `header`, which stays put, then everything under it in the page's scroller, a `ScrollArea` (its scrollbar drawn over the page's edge, taking no width) whose scrolling element carries `data-slot="page-scroll"`; the app's router scrolls it to the top on a new page and restores it on Back and Forward. A page with no header (Settings and Integrations, which title themselves in the content) leaves `header` out and keeps a bare 48px strip in its place, to drag the window by and to keep the content clear of the `WindowBar`. `scrollClassName` styles the scroller.
- **`PageHeader`:** a 48px bar, 24px in from the sides (on Windows its right end stops 16px short of the caption buttons instead, as it reaches the window's top right there): the page's place at the left (a `Breadcrumb`: "Tasks › List", "Projects › Auth Overhaul › Implement OAuth2 PKCE flow") and its actions at the right, 8px apart. Detail pages put the star (`PageFavoriteButton`, filled in `date-today` while on) and the "…" menu (`PageMenuButton`, aligned to the right edge) there. It spans the page even where the content is a capped column. While the sidebar is hidden the `WindowBar` sits over the page's top left, and the header starts after it: `WorkspaceShell` sets `--window-bar-inset` to the bar's width (0 while the sidebar is docked), which the header adds to its left padding. A page's own bar in place of a `PageHeader` does the same. With no `actions` the actions element isn't drawn.
- **`PageToolbar`:** the row under it: `start` (Filter) and `end` (Sort, the view switch, the quiet "New …" button), 32px controls 8px apart, 12px above the content. Toolbar buttons show their value after the label; in compact windows pass `hideValue`.
- **`PageContent`:** the page under them. `list` and `grid` fill the height under the toolbar and pass it on as a flex column, so the `TaskList`, `Board` or `VirtualCardGrid` placed in them fills it and scrolls itself while the header and toolbar stay put (keep the list a direct child, or give a wrapper `display: flex; flex-direction: column; flex: 1; min-height: 0`). `list` keeps rows 16px from the sides, `grid` cards 24px, `home` Home's 800px column (`home-max-width`), centred across the page and, while it fits, down it, and `document` centres a 720px column (`content-max-width`) 48px under the header: notes, tasks, Settings and Integrations. `home` and `document` keep their own height, and the `Page`'s scroller scrolls them whole.

**Base UI:** `Menu` for the "…" menu; the rest is plain markup.
