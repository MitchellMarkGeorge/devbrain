# PageHeader

A page's frame and the parts it is built from, for lists, cards, the calendar, Settings and detail pages. Each page draws its own: the shell holds nothing of the page's, so a page decides whether it has a header and what is in it, and can lay itself out as it needs.

- **`Page`:** what every page in a `WorkspaceShell` renders: its `header`, then everything else in the page's scroller, a `ScrollArea` (its scrollbar drawn over the page's edge, taking no width) whose scrolling element carries `data-slot="page-scroll"`; the app's router scrolls it to the top on a new page and restores it on Back and Forward. Inside a `WorkspaceShell` the header goes **up into the window's title bar**, after the window's controls: `Page` portals it into the shell's slot (`useWorkspaceShell().titleBarContainer`), so it stays put, takes no room in the page's card, and is still the page's own JSX (its menus use the page's state). The page's content then starts at the card's top, and a `PageToolbar` there keeps 12px clear of its edge, whether the page has a header or not (`data-in-shell`). Outside a shell (stories, tests) the page draws its header at its top. A page with no header leaves `header` out: Settings and Integrations, which title themselves in the content, and Home and the calendar, whose content says where you are (Home's greeting, the calendar's month). `scrollClassName` styles the scroller.
- **`PageHeader`:** a bar 24px in from the sides, filling the title bar after the window's controls and as tall as it (`title-bar-height`, 40px; the shell sets `--page-header-height`), or 48px topping the page outside a shell: the page's place at the left (a `Breadcrumb`: "Tasks › List", "Projects › Auth Overhaul › Implement OAuth2 PKCE flow") and its actions at the right, 8px apart. Detail pages put the star (`PageFavoriteButton`, filled in `date-today` while on) and the "…" menu (`PageMenuButton`, aligned to the right edge) there. In the title bar its buttons and links don't drag the window and the rest of it does; on Windows its actions stop short of the caption buttons (both the shell's doing). With no `actions` the actions element isn't drawn.
- **`PageToolbar`:** the row under it: `start` (Filter) and `end` (Sort, the view switch, the quiet "New …" button), 32px controls 8px apart, 12px above the content. Toolbar buttons show their value after the label; in compact windows pass `hideValue`.
- **`PageContent`:** the page under them. `list` and `grid` fill the height under the toolbar and pass it on as a flex column, so the `TaskList`, `Board` or `VirtualCardGrid` placed in them fills it and scrolls itself while the header and toolbar stay put (keep the list a direct child, or give a wrapper `display: flex; flex-direction: column; flex: 1; min-height: 0`). `list` keeps rows 16px from the sides, `grid` cards 24px, `home` Home's 800px column (`home-max-width`), centred across the page and, while it fits, down it, and `document` centres a 720px column (`content-max-width`) 48px under the header: notes, tasks, Settings and Integrations. `home` and `document` keep their own height, and the `Page`'s scroller scrolls them whole.

**Base UI:** `Menu` for the "…" menu; the rest is plain markup.
