# Sidebar

shadcn's Sidebar laid out as in Figma's `Sidebar` symbol: 256px on the page colour (`sidebar`) with a `sidebar-border` hairline at its right, 12px side padding, 16px between blocks.

- **No window row:** the window's controls, search (the search field) and New are in the `WorkspaceShell`'s title bar, above the sidebar, docked or in the drawer. Figma's `SidebarWindowRow` and the switcher row's search and new buttons are superseded.
- **Header:** a row pinned at the top, the sidebar's `header`, straight under the title bar (no padding above it) and 16px above what follows. In the app it is a `SidebarHeader` holding the workspace switcher (`WorkspaceButton`, see `WorkspaceSwitcher`); in the settings, Back to app. The Figma screens' frames add `SidebarHeaderActions` of `SidebarAction`s (28px icon buttons with their shortcut in the tooltip) beside the switcher; the app has none.
- **Items:** every item, in the main navigation and in Recents and Favorites, is the same: 32px, 8px in, 16px icon (or an event's 16px `ColourBar`), 14px label, 8px corners. Items are quiet: `muted-foreground` until hovered (which fills `sidebar-accent`) or current (the current page takes `sidebar-accent` and `sidebar-accent-foreground`, with no outline), so the title bar above leads. The main navigation's items are 2px apart; Recents and Favorites stack edge to edge under Nova's 32px label (12px medium at 70%), which folds the group away when clicked (its chevron shows on hover); groups fold by default, `collapsible={false}` keeps one open.
- **Empty group:** a group that holds nothing can keep its label and say what it would hold, in a `SidebarHint`: 12px `subtle-foreground`, in line with the items' icons, 4px under the label. In the app, Favorites does this before anything is starred; an empty Recents shows nothing.
- **Footer:** the `SyncStatus` line, then Trash, Settings and Help (which opens the keyboard shortcuts), pushed to the bottom.
- **Scrolling:** in a window too short for every item, everything, the footer included, scrolls as one in a `ScrollArea`, whose thin scrollbar shows over the right edge (taking no width from the items). A `header`, at the sidebar's top, stays put above it, casting `shadow-pinned` while anything is scrolled under it.

The sidebar has one layout. Inside a `WorkspaceShell` it is **docked** at the left or **hidden** (the user's choice, ⌘\, at any size; and always in compact windows, below `breakpoint-medium`), and while hidden it opens as a **drawer** over the page: pointing at the sidebar toggle or the window's left edge peeks at it, and clicking the toggle pins it open (or docks it, where the window is wide enough). `WorkspaceShell` does all of this, and the motion (see its guide); the sidebar only renders. Inside the shell it loses its own hairline and background: the card's edge separates it from the page, and it sits on the window's `sidebar` colour. In the drawer it carries `data-drawer`, drops its hairline and takes the drawer's surface (`popover`), its hover and current item filled with 8% of `foreground` over it (its own `sidebar-accent` is tuned for the frame and doesn't show on `popover` in dark), and picking an item closes the drawer.

**Base UI:** No single Base UI part: shadcn's Sidebar layout built from plain markup, with Base UI `Collapsible` for collapsible groups. Active items carry `data-active`.
