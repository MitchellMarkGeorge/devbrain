# Sidebar

shadcn's Sidebar laid out as in Figma's `Sidebar` symbol: 256px on the page colour (`sidebar`) with a `sidebar-border` hairline at its right, 12px side padding, 16px between blocks.

- **No window row:** the window's controls (the title bar's start, the sidebar toggle, back and forward) are in the `WorkspaceShell`'s title bar (`WindowBar`), above the sidebar, docked or in the drawer. Figma's `SidebarWindowRow` is superseded.
- **Header:** a 20px `WorkspaceTile`, the workspace name (14px medium `foreground`) and a 12px up-down chevron that opens the `WorkspaceSwitcher` (the row fills with `sidebar-accent` on hover and `accent` while open); then search and new (`SidebarAction`: 28px icon buttons with their shortcut in the tooltip).
- **Items:** every item, in the main navigation and in Recents and Favorites, is the same: 32px, 8px in, 16px icon (or an event's 16px `ColourBar`), 14px `sidebar-foreground` label, 8px corners. Hover fills `sidebar-accent`; the current page takes `sidebar-accent` with a `border-strong` outline drawn inside (so labels never shift). The main navigation's items are 2px apart; Recents and Favorites stack edge to edge under Nova's 32px label (12px medium at 70%), which folds the group away when clicked (its chevron shows on hover); groups fold by default, `collapsible={false}` keeps one open.
- **Footer:** the `SyncStatus` line, then Trash, Settings and Help, pushed to the bottom.
- **Scrolling:** in a window too short for every item, the workspace switcher row (with search and new), 8px under the sidebar's top, stays put, and everything below it, the footer included, scrolls as one beneath it. It scrolls in a `ScrollArea`, whose thin scrollbar shows over the right edge (taking no width from the items), and while anything is scrolled under the row it casts `shadow-pinned`. The page passes the switcher row as the sidebar's `header` (`<Sidebar header={<SidebarHeader>…</SidebarHeader>}>`).

The sidebar has one layout. Inside a `WorkspaceShell` it is **docked** at the left or **hidden** (the user's choice, ⌘\, at any size; and always in compact windows, below `breakpoint-medium`), and while hidden it opens as a **drawer** over the page: pointing at the sidebar toggle or the window's left edge peeks at it, and clicking the toggle pins it open (or docks it, where the window is wide enough). `WorkspaceShell` does all of this, and the motion (see its guide); the sidebar only renders. Inside the shell it loses its own hairline and background: the card's edge separates it from the page, and it sits on the window's `sidebar` colour. In the drawer it carries `data-drawer`, and picking an item closes the drawer.

**Base UI:** No single Base UI part: shadcn's Sidebar layout built from plain markup, with Base UI `Menu` for the workspace switcher and `Collapsible` for collapsible groups. Active items carry `data-active`.
