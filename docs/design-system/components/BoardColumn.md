# BoardColumn

An open status column on the board, as in the quiet direction: no panel, just a 24px header (the `StatusIcon`, the status name in 14px medium, and a ghost xs "+" button on the right) over its `BoardCard`s, 10px apart (`board-card-gap`), and a dashed 40px "Add task" slot (`border-strong` dashes, 10px corners, a 16px "+" and the label in `muted-foreground`; `add: false` for Completed). Columns sit 24px apart and share the width equally (`Board`).

- `status` is a `TaskStatus`; the heading is its label ("In progress") unless `name` is given.
- **Data.** `tasks`: core's tasks for the status as they are, each with its `id`. Cards are `BoardCard`s that call `onOpen(task)`; `cardProps(task, index)` adds props to one (a static `state` for pictures, a drag handle's props).
- **Each column scrolls on its own.** The `Board` fills the height its container gives it (`flex: 1`: placed straight in a `PageContent`), each column fills the board's height, and its cards scroll under its header, which stays put, in a `ScrollArea` (the scrollbar drawn over the column's edge, taking no width); the dashed slot ends the scrolled cards. The cards are virtualized with `@tanstack/react-virtual` against the column's own scroller: only those in view, and about ten either side, are in the DOM, each measured as it draws since pills wrap. `onEndReached` fires near the last loaded cards (`endReachedThreshold`, 20) to load the column's next page. The drawn cards flow in order (not positioned) in a `role="list"` named for the column, each card itself a `listitem` with `aria-posinset` and `aria-setsize` (of `rowCount`, the column's total, or the loaded cards), so a screen reader says "card 212 of 340". Each card is measured directly; the virtualizer counts `board-card-gap` into every card's offset, and the list draws the same gap. ↑/↓ on a card move to the next, scrolling it into view first; Enter opens it. The story `1,000 cards a column` loads 100 at a time.
- **No due date.** `onDueDateChange(task, date)` gives each undated card its "Set due date" (`BoardCard`'s `onDueDateChange`).
- The designed board shows no count in the header; `count` still adds one after the name when a page wants it.
- The header's "+" and the slot both call `onAdd`. Figma's `QuickAddRow` is the inline row a list group (or a column) can end with once "Add task" is clicked.
