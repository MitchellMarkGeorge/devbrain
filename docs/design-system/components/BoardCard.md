# BoardCard

A task on the board, carrying the same pills as its task row so a task reads the same in both views. It takes core's `TaskListItem` as it is (`task`, typed `BoardCardData`: a `Pick` of the fields it draws) and derives the rest:

- **Title** first, 14px `foreground-strong`, wrapping.
- **Pills** under it, 4px apart, wrapping onto further lines: the synced task's `SourcePill`, the subtask count (`ProgressRing`, as in rows), the task's `link` (an event, as an `EntityPill` with its calendar's bar, or a note), then the project (its folder in the project's colour). All 24px. A name too long for the card ellipsises inside it. A task with no links has no pill line. The key, the link and the project open their `EntityHoverCard` after about 400ms of pointing, from the `PillHoverCardsProvider` around the page, as in a task row.
- **Meta line** last: `PriorityIcon` on the left, the due date on the right, from `useFormat()`'s `formatListDate`, always the date ("Jul 1"; "Dec 2025" when not this year's), and coloured by `dueTone` from the status, the date and `now`: `date-today` for an open task due today, `date-overdue` for an overdue one. A completed task (`TaskStatus.COMPLETED`) keeps its priority, as every task does, so the line always shows.
- **No due date.** An open card without one shows nothing in the date's place at rest. With `onDueDateChange`, a `SetDueDateButton` (a quiet calendar-plus and "Due", named "Set due date") takes the place while the card is pointed at or focused, and stays while its picker is open. A closed or synced card never shows it.
- **Card:** borderless and filled (`card`; `neutral-100` in light, where a borderless card needs a step more to show on white), 12px padding, 8px between its lines, 10px corners. On hover it fills with `accent` (`neutral-800` in dark, Figma's `card-fill-hover`), with no outline: the fill alone shows it. Keyboard focus takes Nova's ring. Completed cards go darker with a dimmed title.
- **Clicks:** a pill opens what it links to; a click anywhere else opens the task (`onClick` doesn't fire for clicks on the pills).

**Clicks that don't open the task.** Its own controls (the status button, the checkbox, its pills, links) and anything it opened, such as the status menu, a date picker or its context menu: those are portalled elsewhere in the page, but React still bubbles their clicks up to the card, which ignores a click whose target is outside it.
