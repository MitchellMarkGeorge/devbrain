# NoteRow

A note in the Notes list, on the shared 48px `ListRow`: the file-text icon, the title ("Untitled" when it has none), its `preview` cut to one line in `muted-foreground` (it shortens first), the pills of what the note links to (its `link`, a task or an event, then its `project`, as `EntityPill`s) and when it was edited (`formatRelative`: "2h ago", "3 days ago") right-aligned in 60px (the widest, "11mo ago", is about 58px). It stays relative on purpose, unlike the task and project lists, which show the date: for a note, how recently it was edited is what matters. The checkbox shows on hover. Its pills open their `EntityHoverCard` after about 400ms of pointing, from the `PillHoverCardsProvider` around the page.

`<NoteRow note={note} onSelect onOpen onOpenEntity />`, where `note` is core's `NoteListItem` as it is (`NoteRowData`: `title`, `preview`, `updatedAt`, `project`, `link`). A click on a pill calls `onOpenEntity` with its summary (pages pass the app's `useOpenEntity()`) and doesn't open the note.
