# Page

A page's frame and the parts it is built from, for lists, cards, the calendar, Settings and detail pages. Each page declares its own: the shell holds nothing of the page's, so a page decides its title, whether it has actions and what they are, and can lay itself out as it needs.

- **`Page`:** what every page in a `WorkspaceShell` renders: its `title`, its `actions`, then everything else in the page's scroller, a `ScrollArea` (its scrollbar drawn over the page's edge, taking no width) whose scrolling element carries `data-slot="page-scroll"`; the app's router scrolls it to the top on a new page and restores it on Back and Forward. Inside a `WorkspaceShell`:
  - the **`title`** (a string: "Tasks", a task's or note's title, "Settings · General") shows in the title bar's search field, which says where you are and opens search: `Page` portals it in (`useWorkspaceShell().locationContainer`). A page still loading leaves it out, and the field reads "Search or jump to…". It also names the window (`document.title`, which Electron gives it: the Window menu, Mission Control); the next page names it in turn (a screen outside the shell, like Create workspace, leaves it as it was). There are no breadcrumbs.
  - the **`actions`** (a detail page's star, `PageFavoriteButton`, filled in `date-today` while on, and "…" menu, `PageMenuButton`, aligned to the right edge) go at the title bar's end, 8px apart and 24px in from the side: `Page` portals them into the shell's slot (`useWorkspaceShell().titleBarContainer`), so they stay put, take no room in the page's card, and are still the page's own JSX (their menus use the page's state). The bar is as tall as the title bar (the shell sets `--page-actions-height`), its buttons don't drag the window and the rest of it does, and on Windows it stops short of the caption buttons (both the shell's doing). A page with no actions leaves `actions` out.

  The page's content starts at the card's top, and a `PageToolbar` there keeps 12px clear of its edge (`data-in-shell`). Outside a shell (stories, tests) the page draws its actions in a 48px bar at its top, and the title isn't drawn. `scrollClassName` styles the scroller.

- **`PageToolbar`:** the row at the page's top: `start` (which items are shown: Status and the filters) and `end` (how they are drawn: Display); a list page fills both with the `ListToolbar` pieces. The start group takes the room the end group leaves, and the toolbar stays one row, 32px controls 8px apart, 12px above the content. Toolbar buttons show their value after the label; in compact windows pass `hideValue`. Toolbar buttons are `outline` (`variant="outline"`): the page's colour inside a `border` line, filling with `accent` on hover and while open, so in dark they don't read as the rows' pills.
- **`PageContent`:** the page under them. `list` and `grid` fill the height under the toolbar and pass it on as a flex column, so the `TaskList`, `Board` or `VirtualCardGrid` placed in them fills it and scrolls itself while the toolbar stays put (keep the list a direct child, or give a wrapper `display: flex; flex-direction: column; flex: 1; min-height: 0`). `list` keeps rows 16px from the sides, `grid` cards 24px, `home` Home's 800px column (`home-max-width`), centred across the page and, while it fits, down it, and `document` centres a 720px column (`content-max-width`) 48px down: notes, tasks, Settings and Integrations. `home` and `document` keep their own height, and the `Page`'s scroller scrolls them whole.

**Base UI:** `Menu` for the "…" menu; the rest is plain markup.
