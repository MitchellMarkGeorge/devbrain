# WorkspaceTile

A workspace's letter on the 900 step of its colour (white letter above 9:1): 20px with 6px corners and a 12px medium letter in the sidebar (Figma's `WorkspaceTile`), 32px with 8px corners and a 14px letter in the workspace list (`WorkspaceListItem`), and the same slope between and around them up to 40px; 60–72px with 16px corners on onboarding.

- `letter`, `color` (a swatch name), `size`. The letter is `white`, 9:1 or more on every 900 step.
