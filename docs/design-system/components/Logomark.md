# Logomark

The DevBrain mark: a circle cut by diagonal bands, 40×48. It is an image asset in the Logos group, never redrawn.

- On dark grounds (`background` in dark) use `devbrain-logomark.svg` (white). On light grounds use `devbrain-logomark-dark.svg` (`neutral-950`).
- Sizes: 20×24 beside the name in a lockup, 40×48 above onboarding titles, 60×72 pulsing on the splash screen.
- Lockup: the mark, `space-3` gap, then "DevBrain" in Inter semibold in `foreground-strong`. There is no separate wordmark file.
- Keep clear space of at least `space-2` around the mark. Do not recolour it with status or swatch colours.
