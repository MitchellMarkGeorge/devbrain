# GroupHeading

A group in the grouped list. No band and no line: 24px of space above it, a chevron, a 32px tile (the group's icon in its colour, or an event's colour bar), the title (14px medium) over a subtitle (12px `muted-foreground`), an xs "Add task" button, the done/total count and an 80px progress bar.

- Group by due date ("Overdue · Oldest due Sep 28"), by what tasks link to ("Mitchell / Jesse 1:1 · Event · Today 10:00 AM") or by project. `collapsed` hides the Add button and points the chevron right.
