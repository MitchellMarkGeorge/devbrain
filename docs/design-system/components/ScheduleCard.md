# ScheduleCard

Figma's `ScheduleCard`: an upcoming event on Home's "Today's schedule". A 60px `card` with the `edge` outline and 14px corners, 12px/16px padding and 12px gaps: the calendar's 3×36 `ColourBar`, the title (14px medium `foreground-strong`) over "In 12 min · 9:00 AM" (12px `subtle-foreground`, the time until it starts in `date-today` when soon, or the duration), and an xs "Join" button with a video icon when it has a meeting link. `ScheduleCardSkeleton` is its loading ghost.
