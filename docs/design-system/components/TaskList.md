# TaskList

A virtualized list of `TaskRow`s that handles selection: rows selected next to each other join into one block, every row shows its checkbox while anything is selected, and the `SelectionBar` floats over the bottom of the list.

- **Data.** `tasks` (core's `TaskListItem`s as they are, `TaskRowData[]`) for a flat list, or `groups` for the grouped view, one or the other (the props type rejects both, or neither): each `{ id, heading, tasks, collapsed?, footer? }`, where `heading` is a `GroupHeading` (and anything drawn under it, such as an event's `EventRow`) and `footer` a "Show more" row while the group has more to load.
- **Its own scroller.** The list fills the height its container gives it (`flex: 1` in a column: placed straight in a `PageContent layout="list"`, or a set height) and scrolls its rows there, under the page header, which never scrolls away. With no height to fill it grows to its rows and draws them all, which suits a short list (a task's subtasks). Virtualized with `@tanstack/react-virtual` against itself: only the rows in view, and about ten either side, are in the DOM, so 5,000 tasks draw about 30 rows. The drawn rows are in normal flow, in index order, not positioned: a box the whole list's height holds them, padded at the top by the space the rows above them would take, so the scrollbar is the whole list's and screen readers meet the rows in order. Every row is measured as it draws; until then a row is placed at `row-height` (48px, and a heading also `group-gap` above it), read from the generated `src/renderer/styles/tokens.ts`.
- **Grouped view.** Headings and rows are one flat list. Every heading sticks to the top of the list as it reaches it, and the next one covers it; the heading of the group at the top of the view is kept drawn ahead of the rows, so it stays stuck (on `--list-ground`, the page's `background` by default; set it on a card or sheet), with `group-gap` above every heading but the first. Collapsing a group (`collapsed`) leaves its rows out of the list.
- **Loading more.** `onEndReached` fires once the last drawn row comes within `endReachedThreshold` (20) rows of the end, once for each length of the list: call `fetchNextPage()` there.
- **Focus and selection are kept as task ids**, never as DOM focus alone, so they survive a row scrolling out of view and unmounting. `selected` / `defaultSelected` / `onSelectedChange` take the selected tasks' `id`s (`string[]`); `focusedId` / `onFocusedIdChange` the focused row's (the first row by default). Moving focus to a row off screen calls `scrollToIndex` first, then focuses it once it is drawn; in the grouped view it lands below the stuck heading, not under it. The list is one tab stop: the focused row, or the list itself while that row is scrolled away, which brings it back.
- **Accessibility.** `role="grid"` with `aria-rowcount` (`rowCount`, the total a count gives, or the loaded rows and headings) and `aria-rowindex` on every drawn row and heading, so a screen reader announces "row 212 of 340" with 30 rows in the DOM. Browser find (⌘F) sees only drawn rows; ⌘K searches everything.
- **Pictures.** `states` (`{taskIndex: 'hover' | 'focus'}`), `menuOpenAt` (a row with its status menu open), `subtasks` (indent every row), `selectionBar: false` to leave the bar to the page.
- **Keys.** The list binds none. Its `ref` (`TaskListHandle`) does what the keys do: `moveFocus(±n)` (scrolling the row into view first), `focusFirst`/`focusLast`, `toggleSelected`, `clearSelection`, `openStatusMenu` on the focused row, and `focusedItem()`/`selectedItems()` for commands that act on rows. The app's keymap drives it through `useListCommands(ref, actions)` (`src/renderer/keyboard/list-commands.ts`) inside a `<KeyScope name="list">`: ↑/↓, Home/End, `X`, Esc (clear the selection), `S`, `1`–`3`, ↵, `F` and ⌘⌫, the last four through the page's `open`, `setStatus`, `toggleFavorite` and `trash`. A row command acts on the selected rows, or the focused row when none is selected. The stories mount the keyboard layer the same way.
- The status menu that is open is the list's (`menuOpenAt` opens one at first, for pictures).
- Stories: `1,000 rows` (loaded 100 at a time from `onEndReached`) and `1,000 rows, grouped` (sticky headings), each filling a page's content area under its header.
