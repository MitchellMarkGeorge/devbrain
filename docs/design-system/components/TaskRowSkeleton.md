# TaskRowSkeleton

A `TaskRow` while the list loads: 48px of pulsing `Skeleton` blocks with 16px side padding and 12px between them: a 16px status square, a 12px-tall title bar (`titleWidth`, 240px by default), a 48px key, then on the right a 104 × 20px pill, a 16px priority and a 40px date. Stack them with no gap and vary `titleWidth` from row to row.
