# Sheet

The details panel for a task, an event or a note (shadcn's Sheet), 380px (`sheet-width`), on `popover`. `size="wide"` (480px) exists, but no page uses it: the shell's docked column is `sheet-width` wide, so a wider docked sheet needs the column widened with it.

- **Header.** A 48px bar (16px in on the left, 12px on the right) with the panel's name in 14px medium `foreground` ("Task", "Event", "Note"; an event's note has "‹ Event" there instead, a ghost xs button back to the event), then "open as page" and close as quiet 24px icon buttons with 12px icons. It stays put while the body scrolls under it.
- **Body** (`SheetBody`): a `ScrollArea` filling the height under the header, so its thin scrollbar starts below the header. The content sits 8px under the header, 16px in from the sides, 20px off the bottom, its blocks 16px apart. It opens with the item's name, `SheetItemTitle` (16px medium `foreground-strong`; an event's calendar colour as a 3px bar before it), then a synced item's `SyncedBadge`, the properties as `DetailRow`s (or `ReadOnlyRow`s for a synced item, followed by the sync hint and "Open in Linear" / "Detach…"), a `SheetDivider` (`border-strong`), the description, and `DetailSection`s: Subtasks or Linked tasks (`LinkedTaskRow`s), Task note (a stacked `TaskNoteCard`) or Event note (an `AddSlot` while there is none).
- **Docked by default.** The sheet opens as a card of its own beside the page's, under the title bar: the page colour, a `border` hairline, `radius-lg` corners and no shadow, 8px from the page's card and the window's edge, as the page's card is drawn (`WorkspaceShell`). The header takes the sheet's own surface (`background: inherit`), so it reads as one card. The page narrows to make room, so nothing the user was looking at is hidden: board columns narrow, the week grid's day columns narrow (all its days stay: seven, or five with Hide weekends on), and the calendar's right panel (mini calendar, Today, Tomorrow) hides while a sheet is open. Opening another item switches the sheet's content: one sheet stays open, so going from an event to its note or a linked task slides nothing.
- **On Windows** the sheet starts under the title bar (`title-bar-height`), docked or floating: Windows draws its caption buttons over the window's top right, and the sheet's own close would sit beside theirs (README "Window frame"). Docked, it is under the title bar on every platform; floating, the overlay's `top` is `--title-bar-height` there.
- **Overlay in compact windows.** Below `breakpoint-medium` (1080px), where docking would leave too little page, the sheet floats over the page instead and scrolls inside itself: inset 8px, 14px corners, the `edge` outline and `shadow-2xl` (`overlay`). This is how the Figma component sheet (TaskSheet, EventSheet) is drawn on its own.
- **Motion.** Both slide in from the right and out again in `duration-slow` on `ease-surface`. Docked, the sheet rides its column (WorkspaceShell slides the column's width), so the page narrows and widens with it and the two edges stay together; as an overlay it slides over the page while the `backdrop` fades. Nothing moves under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **A note's sheet** opens with the note's title, "Edited …" in `subtle-foreground` under it, its project pill, a `SheetDivider`, the body in the editor and Mentioned in. The note of an event has the meeting over all that: a strip with the event's colour bar, its title in 14px medium, "how soon · times · place" in 12px `muted-foreground` (how soon in `date-today` within the hour; how a series repeats when no one occurrence is meant) and an xs Join button, closed by a `border` hairline; and a footer under the body, "Type / for blocks" and "Notes save as you type" in 12px `subtle-foreground` over a `border` hairline.
- Use for Task Details from the board, a note's page or the calendar; Event Details and an event's note from the calendar; a task's note from the task's page. Dialogs opened from a sheet still float over everything.
- **In the app a page draws its sheet with `useSheet().render()`** (`src/renderer/shared/hooks`), which picks docked or floating and the item's content, and closes the same way everywhere (docs/renderer/routing.md, "Sheets"). `Sheet` itself stays controlled.

**No swipe to dismiss.** A drag across the sheet selects its text (a note, a description), so the popup carries `data-base-ui-swipe-ignore` and the drawer's swipe never starts; ×, Esc and, floating, a click on the backdrop close it.

**Base UI:** `Drawer` with `swipeDirection="right"` (shadcn's Sheet becomes Base UI's Drawer): `Root`, `Portal`, `Backdrop`, `Popup`, `Title`, `Close`; the body is a `ScrollArea`. Docked, it renders without the `Backdrop` and the page shrinks beside it; as an overlay it is modal. Styles key off `data-open`, `data-starting-style` / `data-ending-style`, `data-swiping`.
