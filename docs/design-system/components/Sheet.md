# Sheet

The details panel for a task or an event (shadcn's Sheet), 380px (`sheet-width`) for task and event details, 480px for an event's note (`size="wide"`), on `popover`.

- **Header.** A 48px bar (16px in on the left, 12px on the right) with the panel's name in 14px medium `foreground` ("Task", "Event"), then "open as page" and close as quiet 24px icon buttons with 12px icons. It stays put while the body scrolls under it.
- **Body** (`SheetBody`): a `ScrollArea` filling the height under the header, so its thin scrollbar starts below the header. The content sits 8px under the header, 16px in from the sides, 20px off the bottom, its blocks 16px apart. It opens with the item's name, `SheetItemTitle` (16px medium `foreground-strong`; an event's calendar colour as a 3px bar before it), then a synced item's `SyncedBadge`, the properties as `DetailRow`s (or `ReadOnlyRow`s for a synced item, followed by the sync hint and "Open in Linear" / "Detach…"), a `SheetDivider` (`border-strong`), the description, and `DetailSection`s: Subtasks or Linked tasks (`LinkedTaskRow`s), Task note (a stacked `TaskNoteCard`) or Event note (an `AddSlot` while there is none).
- **Docked by default.** The sheet opens as a full-height column on the right, separated from the page by a `border` line, with square corners and no shadow. The page narrows to make room, so nothing the user was looking at is hidden: board columns narrow, the week grid's day columns narrow (all seven days stay), and the calendar's right panel (mini calendar, Today, Tomorrow) hides while a sheet is open. Opening another item switches the sheet's content.
- **On Windows** the sheet starts under the 48px title-bar strip, docked or floating: Windows draws its caption buttons over the window's top right, and the sheet's own close would sit beside theirs (README "Window frame"). Docked, `AppShell` gives the header a row of its own across the window and the sheet's column starts under it.
- **Overlay in compact windows.** Below `breakpoint-medium` (1080px), where docking would leave too little page, the sheet floats over the page instead and scrolls inside itself: inset 8px, 14px corners, the `edge` outline and `shadow-2xl` (`overlay`). This is how the Figma component sheet (TaskSheet, EventSheet) is drawn on its own.
- **Motion.** Both slide in from the right and out again in `duration-slow` on `ease-surface`. Docked, the sheet rides its column (AppShell slides the column's width), so the page narrows and widens with it and the two edges stay together; as an overlay it slides over the page while the `backdrop` fades. Nothing moves under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Use for Task Details from the board or a list, and Event Details or an event's note from the calendar. Dialogs opened from a sheet still float over everything.

**Base UI:** `Drawer` with `swipeDirection="right"` (shadcn's Sheet becomes Base UI's Drawer): `Root`, `Portal`, `Backdrop`, `Popup`, `Title`, `Close`; the body is a `ScrollArea`. Docked, it renders without the `Backdrop` and the page shrinks beside it; as an overlay it is modal. Styles key off `data-open`, `data-starting-style` / `data-ending-style`, `data-swiping`.
