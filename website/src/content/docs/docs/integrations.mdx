DevBrain can mirror **Linear** and **Google Calendar** into a workspace, so the work you track
elsewhere sits in the same lists, boards and calendar as your own. Connect them in **Settings ›
Integrations** (press <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>I</kbd>). Each workspace has its own connections.

## How sync works

- **It's one way.** DevBrain reads from Linear and Google, and never writes back: not a status, a
  comment or an RSVP. Mirrored items are **read-only** in DevBrain, with a badge saying where they
  came from; change them in Linear or Google Calendar.
- **They're still yours to link.** Link a mirrored task or event to your notes and tasks, give it a
  note, favourite it, search it.
- **There's no server in between.** The app talks to Linear and Google directly, from your computer.
  Your sign-ins are encrypted with your computer's keychain, stored in the workspace, and never sent
  anywhere but Linear or Google.
- **Only the open workspace syncs.** Another workspace catches up when you open it.

### When it syncs

- Every **five minutes** while the workspace is open.
- When the workspace opens, when you come back to DevBrain's window, and when your computer wakes
  (if it hasn't synced in the last minute).
- When you turn an account or source on, reconnect it, or change which calendars to sync.
- When you ask: **Workspace › Sync Now** in the menu bar, or <kbd>⌘⇧S</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl Shift
  S</kbd>).

The bottom of the sidebar shows how it's going: "Synced 2 min ago", "Syncing…", "Sync failed" (with
a button to try again), "Offline", or "Paused" when a service asks DevBrain to slow down. After a
failure DevBrain tries again by itself, waiting a little longer each time.

## Linear

### Connecting

1. In Linear, create a personal API key: **Settings › Security & access**.
2. In DevBrain, choose **Connect** next to Linear, and paste it in **Personal API key**.
3. Choose **Check Key and Connect**. DevBrain checks the key with Linear, then stores it encrypted.
   It's never shown again.

### What syncs

- The issues **assigned to you** that are open, and those completed or cancelled in the last 30
  days. They appear as [tasks](/docs/tasks/#tasks-from-linear), with their key (such as
  **ENG-124**).
- Their **sub-issues**, as subtasks. A sub-issue whose parent isn't assigned to you is a task of its
  own.
- The **Linear projects** of those issues, as [projects](/docs/projects/#projects-from-linear).
- Comments, attachments, labels, cycles, and issues assigned to others are not brought in.

Linear's statuses map to DevBrain's: Triage, Backlog and Todo-type states become **Not started**,
started states **In progress**, done states **Completed**, and cancelled or duplicate issues
**Cancelled**. The state's own name (such as "In Review") is shown on the task. Urgent and High
priority become **High**; an issue with no priority shows **No priority**.

When an open issue is reassigned to someone else or deleted in Linear, it leaves DevBrain within
half an hour, or at once with **Sync Now**. Its notes stay, and come back to it if it's assigned to
you again.

### Editing a Linear task in DevBrain

**Detach from Linear** in a task's **…** menu makes it your own copy: editable, and no longer
updated by Linear. **Reattach to Linear** brings Linear's version back, replacing your edits.

## Google Calendar

### Connecting

1. Choose **Connect** next to Google Calendar. Google's sign-in page opens in your web browser.
2. Sign in, and allow DevBrain to **read** your calendars. DevBrain only asks for read-only access.
3. Back in DevBrain, the account is connected. You can close the browser tab.

If you don't finish signing in within five minutes, or choose **Cancel**, nothing is connected.

### Choosing calendars

At first only your **primary** calendar syncs. To add others, choose **Choose Calendars** on the
account's **Events** row, and tick the ones you want. Calendars you untick, and any new ones Google
adds later, stay in Google. Hide a synced calendar for a while with the calendar's
[Filter](/docs/calendar/#which-calendars-show) instead.

### What syncs

- Events from **30 days ago onward**, with no limit into the future. Older events load when you go
  back to them in the calendar.
- Regular events, focus time, out of office, and events from Gmail. Working-location and birthday
  events are left out.
- Events you declined, shown faded.

They're [read-only in the calendar](/docs/calendar/#google-calendar-events), but you can take
notes on them and link tasks to them. If a meeting you've written notes for is cancelled, its note
is kept.

## Turning sync off and on

Each account has a switch, and so does each of its sources (**Tasks** for Linear, **Events** for
Google). Turning one off pauses it: what it brought in stays as it is, and its sign-in is kept.
Turning it back on picks up where it left off.

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect…** on an account stops syncing it and deletes its key or sign-in from your computer
(DevBrain also asks Linear or Google to revoke it). Choose what happens to what it brought in:

- **Keep as local copies** (the default): the items stay in DevBrain as your own. They stop syncing
  and become editable.
- **Remove them**: the items are deleted, except those a note or task links to (or that hold a
  task that's kept), which stay as local copies.

If you connect the same account again later, DevBrain offers to **Reattach** the local copies so
they sync again (replacing any changes you made to them), or to **Keep Local**.

## When an account needs reconnecting

If Linear or Google rejects the saved key or sign-in (you revoked it, or it expired), the account
shows **Needs reconnect** and stops syncing. What it already brought in stays. Choose **Update API
Key** (Linear) or **Reconnect** (Google) to carry on.

This also happens when you [move a workspace to another computer](/docs/workspaces/#moving-a-workspace-to-another-computer):
sign-ins are encrypted with a key in your computer's own keychain, so another computer can't read
them. Your accounts, their settings and what they synced are all kept; reconnect each to resume.
