DevBrain can mirror [**Linear**](/docs/integrations/linear/) and
[**Google Calendar**](/docs/integrations/google-calendar/) into a workspace, so the work you track
elsewhere sits in the same lists, boards and calendar as your own. Connect them in **Settings ›
Integrations** (press <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>I</kbd>). Each workspace has its own connections.

## How sync works

- **It's one way.** DevBrain reads from Linear and Google, and never writes back: not a status, a
  comment or an RSVP. Mirrored items are **read-only** in DevBrain, with a badge saying where they
  came from; change them in Linear or Google Calendar.
- **They're still yours to link.** Link a mirrored task or event to your notes and tasks, give it a
  note, favourite it, search it.
- **There's no server in between.** The app talks to Linear and Google directly, from your computer.
  Your sign-ins are encrypted with your computer's keychain, stored in the workspace, and never sent
  anywhere but Linear or Google.
- **Only the open workspace syncs.** Another workspace catches up when you open it.

### When it syncs

- Every **five minutes** while the workspace is open.
- When the workspace opens, when you come back to DevBrain's window, and when your computer wakes
  (if it hasn't synced in the last minute).
- When you turn an account or source on, reconnect it, or change which calendars to sync.
- When you ask: **Workspace › Sync Now** in the menu bar, or <kbd>⌘⇧S</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl Shift
  S</kbd>).

The bottom of the sidebar shows how it's going: "Synced 2 min ago", "Syncing…", "Sync failed" (with
a button to try again), "Offline", or "Paused" when a service asks DevBrain to slow down. After a
failure DevBrain tries again by itself, waiting a little longer each time.

## Connecting an account

Each service has its own page, with how to connect it and exactly what it brings in:

- [**Linear**](/docs/integrations/linear/): the issues assigned to you, as tasks, with their
  projects. Connected with a personal API key.
- [**Google Calendar**](/docs/integrations/google-calendar/): the events of the calendars you
  choose. Connected by signing in with Google in your browser.

You can connect more than one account of each, such as a work and a personal Google account. Each
account card on the page shows its status (**Connected**, **Needs reconnect** or **Off**), when it
last synced, and its sources: **Tasks** for Linear, **Events** for Google Calendar.

## Turning sync off and on

Each account has a switch, and so does each of its sources (**Tasks** for Linear, **Events** for
Google). Turning one off pauses it: what it brought in stays as it is, and its sign-in is kept.
Turning it back on picks up where it left off.

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect…** on an account stops syncing it and deletes its key or sign-in from your computer
(DevBrain also asks Linear or Google to revoke it). Choose what happens to what it brought in:

- **Keep as local copies** (the default): the items stay in DevBrain as your own. They stop syncing
  and become editable.
- **Remove them**: the items are deleted, except those a note or task links to (or that hold a
  task that's kept), which stay as local copies.

If you connect the same account again later, DevBrain offers to **Reattach** the local copies so
they sync again (replacing any changes you made to them), or to **Keep Local**.

## When an account needs reconnecting

If Linear or Google rejects the saved key or sign-in (you revoked it, or it expired), the account
shows **Needs reconnect** and stops syncing. What it already brought in stays. Choose **Update API
Key** ([Linear](/docs/integrations/linear/#updating-the-key)) or **Reconnect**
([Google](/docs/integrations/google-calendar/#reconnecting)) to carry on.

This also happens when you [move a workspace to another computer](/docs/workspaces/#moving-a-workspace-to-another-computer):
sign-ins are encrypted with a key in your computer's own keychain, so another computer can't read
them. Your accounts, their settings and what they synced are all kept; reconnect each to resume.
