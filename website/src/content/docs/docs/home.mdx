Home is the page DevBrain opens to (you can pick another **Start page** in
[Settings](/docs/settings/#preferences)). It tells you what matters right now, and gives you a box to
write something down before you forget it.

## What Home shows

- **A greeting and a sentence about your day**, such as "You have 3 tasks today, and 1 is overdue."
  or "Standup starts in 12 minutes."
- **The capture box** (below).
- **One section**, whichever needs you soonest, with up to three rows:
  - **Meetings**: today's next meetings, counting down. A **Join** button appears five minutes
    before a meeting with a link starts.
  - **Tasks**: open tasks due today or overdue. Tick them off or give them a date right here.
  - **Projects due soon** (those due within a week) or, when none are, **Recent projects** (the
    ones you've been working on), with
    their progress.
  - **Recent notes**.
  - **Get started**, in a new workspace: buttons to connect Google Calendar and Linear, and to
    start a project.

If an integration needs you to sign in again, the bottom of Home says so, with a **Reconnect**
button.

## Quick capture

Pick **Task**, **Note** or **Event**, type one line and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. DevBrain reads the
details out of what you type, highlights what it understood, and fills in the pickers below the
line. Anything you choose in a picker yourself wins over what it read.

### Tasks

Type the task, with a date and a project if you like:

| You type                                   | DevBrain makes                                   |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| `Draft Q3 roadmap tomorrow`                | "Draft Q3 roadmap", due tomorrow                 |
| `Fix the login redirect #Website`          | "Fix the login redirect", in the Website project |
| `Send the invoice by next friday #Billing` | "Send the invoice", due next Friday, in Billing  |
| `Renew the domain in 3 days`               | "Renew the domain", due three days from today    |

- **`#Project`** files the task in a project: `#` followed by an existing project's whole title,
  in any case (`#billing v2`). It never creates a project, and a `#` in the middle of a word is left
  alone.
- **A date** can be "today", "tomorrow", a weekday with "next" or "on" ("next friday"), "in 3
  days", or a date like "Jun 30". A short weekday on its own ("wed") is left as part of the title,
  as it so often is a word in one. "on", "by" or "due" before the date is taken with it.
- With no date, the task gets your **Default due date**; its priority is the **Default priority**
  ([Settings › Tasks](/docs/settings/#tasks)), or whatever you pick in the **Priority** picker.

### Notes

Type a title (with `#Project` to file it) and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>: the note is created and opens,
ready to write in.

### Events

Type what's happening and when, such as `Design review tomorrow at 3pm` or
`Lunch with Sam on friday at 12:30`. The event goes in the calendar you choose in the **Calendar** picker and
lasts as long as the length picker says (the **Default event length** setting at first). With no
time, it starts at the next half hour today, or at 9 AM on another day.
