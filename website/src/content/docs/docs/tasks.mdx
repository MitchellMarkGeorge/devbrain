Tasks are the heart of DevBrain: your own, and the [Linear](/docs/integrations/linear/) issues
assigned to you, in the same lists.

## What a task has

| Field             | Values                                                                           |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Status**        | Not started, In progress, Completed, Cancelled                                   |
| **Priority**      | High, Medium, Low (Linear issues with no priority show **No priority**)          |
| **Start date**    | Optional: when you mean to start                                                 |
| **Due date**      | Optional. Today's are highlighted; an open task's date turns red once it's past  |
| **Project**       | The [project](/docs/projects/) it belongs to, if any                             |
| **Description**   | Rich text, written in the same [editor](/docs/notes/#the-editor) as notes        |
| **Subtasks**      | Tasks inside the task, with their own status and dates                           |
| **Event or note** | One [calendar event](/docs/calendar/) or one [note](/docs/notes/) it's linked to |
| **Pull request**  | A link to the task's pull request                                                |

Cancelled closes a task without completing it: it doesn't count toward a project's progress.

## Creating tasks

- **New task** in the title bar's **+** menu, or press the key <kbd>C</kbd> anywhere. On the Tasks
  page filtered to a project, or on a project's page, the new task goes in that project.
- On [Home](/docs/home/#quick-capture), type one line such as `Fix the login redirect tomorrow
#Website`.
- The **+** on a group's heading or a board column, which fills in that group's status, priority or
  project for you.
- From what you're writing in a note or a description: type **<kbd>/</kbd>task** to open **New
  task**, or select text and choose **Turn into task**. A task made in a note is linked to that note
  (see [Tasks from a note](/docs/notes/#tasks-from-a-note)).

The **New task** dialog has the **Title**, a description, and buttons for the status, priority, due
date and project. Its **…** button adds a start date, or a list of subtasks to create with it.
Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> in the title, or <kbd>⌘ Enter</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl Enter</kbd>) anywhere, to
create it. Turn on **Create more** to keep the dialog open for the next one.

New tasks start as **Not started**, with the **Default priority** and **Default due date** from
[Settings › Tasks](/docs/settings/#tasks).

### Picking dates

A date picker has quick picks (**Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Next week**), a calendar, and a field
that reads what you type: "tomorrow", "next fri", "Jun 30".

## A task's page

Click a task in a list to open its page.

- **The title**: click to edit; <kbd>Enter</kbd> saves and <kbd>Esc</kbd> puts it back.
- **The properties** under it: click any to change it. **+ Add** adds a **Parent task**, an
  **Event or note**, or a **Pull request**.
- **The description**, saved as you type.
- **Subtasks**, with a ring showing how many are completed. **Add** creates one.
- **Task note**: **Add a task note** makes a note named after the task, for the working notes that
  don't belong in the description, and opens it beside the task.

The title bar's **…** menu has the rest:

- **Convert to project**: the task becomes a project, its subtasks become the project's tasks and
  its task note moves to the project. The task itself goes to the trash.
- **Link to event or note…**
- **Make subtask of…**, or **Make top-level task** for a subtask.
- **Duplicate** (<kbd>⌘D</kbd>): a copy, "Copy of …", with its subtasks.
- **Copy as Markdown**
- **Move to trash** (<kbd>⌘⌫</kbd>)

### Subtasks

A subtask is a task in its own right: it has its own page, status, priority and dates, and can have
subtasks of its own. Add one from the parent's **Subtasks** section, or move an existing task under
another with **Make subtask of…**. A subtask is in its parent's project and shares the parent's
linked event or note.

Lists show a parent's progress as a small badge, such as **1/3**: its completed subtasks out of all
of them, leaving out cancelled ones.

### Linking to notes and events

A task can be linked to **one** event or **one** note: the meeting it came out of, or the spec it
implements. Choose **+ Add › Event or note**, then pick from recent and upcoming events and recently
edited notes, or search. The link shows as a pill on the task; click it to open the event or note,
or to change or remove the link.

The note or event sees the link too: an event's sheet lists its **Linked tasks**, and a note shows
the tasks made from it (see [Notes](/docs/notes/#tasks-from-a-note)).

### Pull requests

Add a pull request's link with **+ Add › Pull request**. A GitHub link shows as `repo#123`; click
it to **Open pull request**, **Edit link…** or **Remove pull request**. It's a link only: DevBrain
doesn't read the pull request's state from GitHub.

## The Tasks page

The toolbar has **Filter** and (in the grouped view) **Group by** on the left, and **Sort** and the
view switch on the right.

### Views

- **List**: every task in one list, each subtask indented under its parent, with a line joining
  them. The sort orders the top-level tasks, and each task's subtasks under it. Click a task's
  subtask count, such as **1/3**, or press <kbd>→</kbd> and <kbd>←</kbd>, to show and hide its
  subtasks; a task with more than 50 starts with them hidden. A subtask whose parent isn't in the
  list, because the filter leaves it out, shows its parent's name instead.
- **Grouped**: the same list in groups, by **Project**, **Status**, **Priority** or **Due date**
  (Overdue, Today, This week, Later, No due date). Click a group's heading to fold it.
- **Board**: a column for each status. Drag a card to another column to change its status, or pick
  it up with <kbd>Space</kbd>, move it with <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> and drop it
  with <kbd>Space</kbd>. Clicking a card opens it in a sheet beside the board, so you keep your place.

### Filtering and sorting

**Filter** narrows the list by:

- **Status**: Open (Not started and In progress), All, or one status. Whether completed tasks show
  by default is the **Show completed tasks** setting.
- **Priority**: High, Medium, Low or No priority.
- **Project**: one project, or **No project**.
- **Due**: Overdue, Today, This week (the next seven days) or No due date.
- **Source**: **Local** (your own tasks) or **Synced** (from Linear).

The button shows what's on, such as "Open · High"; **Clear filters** resets it.

**Sort** orders the list (not the board, whose columns follow the status) by **Priority**, **Due
date**, **Status**, **Last updated** or **Created**, ascending or descending.

Your filters, sort and view are part of the page's address, so **Back** and **Forward** step
through them, and DevBrain reopens the page as you left it.

### Working with many tasks

In the list and grouped views, tick the box at the start of a row, or press <kbd>X</kbd> on it, to
select it. The bar that appears changes the **Status**, **Priority**, **Due date** or project
(**Move**) of every selected task at once, or favourites or trashes them.

Right-click a task for its menu: open it, change its status, priority or dates, favourite it, open
its parent or project, or move it to the trash.

With the keyboard, use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to move through the list and <kbd>Enter</kbd>
to open a task. Then <kbd>1</kbd>, <kbd>2</kbd> and <kbd>3</kbd> set it to Not started, In progress
or Completed, <kbd>F</kbd> favourites it and <kbd>⌘⌫</kbd> moves it to the trash. See all the
[keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Tasks from Linear

Issues assigned to you in Linear appear as tasks, with their key (such as **ENG-124**) on them.
They're **read-only**: their title, description, status, priority, dates and project come from
Linear, and Linear's own status name (such as "In Review") is shown. Change them in Linear; **Open
in Linear** is in the **…** menu.

You can still link them to notes and events, give them a task note, and favourite them.

To edit one in DevBrain anyway, choose **Detach from Linear**: it becomes your own copy, and Linear
stops updating it. **Reattach to Linear** brings Linear's version back, replacing your edits. See
[Integrations](/docs/integrations/linear/) for what syncs.

## The trash

**Move to trash** (<kbd>⌘⌫</kbd>) takes a task, with its subtasks and the notes linked to them, out
of your lists. DevBrain asks first, listing the subtasks and notes that go with it (turn that off
with **Ask before moving to trash** in [Settings › Preferences](/docs/settings/#preferences)), and
the **Undo** button in the message that
follows, or <kbd>⌘Z</kbd>, brings it straight back.

**Trash**, at the bottom of the sidebar, holds every task, project and note you've trashed, until
you delete it. Nothing is emptied automatically.

- **Restore** puts an item back where it was.
- **Delete** (**Delete permanently**) removes it for good. Deleting a task deletes its subtasks;
  its task note is kept.
- **Empty Trash** deletes everything in it.
- Open a trashed item to read it; restore it to change it.

Items in the trash aren't [searched](/docs/search/). Tasks synced from Linear can't be trashed:
they leave when they leave Linear, or when you [disconnect](/docs/integrations/#disconnecting).
