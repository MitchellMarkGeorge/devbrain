A project gathers the tasks and notes for one goal, such as a launch, a migration or a client, and
shows how far along it is.

## The Projects page

<DocsScreenshot
  page="projects"
  alt="The Projects page as cards: each project's name, due date, progress bar with tasks done, and status"
  focusX={0}
/>

Show your projects as a **List** or as **Cards**. Each shows its colour, status, progress (the
share of its tasks completed) and its due date, or the day it was completed.

- **Filter** by status (Active by default, so projects that are not started, on hold or completed
  are hidden until you pick **All** or that status) and by source (**Local** or **Synced** from
  Linear).
- **Sort** by **Due date** (the default, soonest first, projects with no due date last),
  **Status**, **Last updated** or **Created**.
- Right-click a project to open it, favourite it or move it to the trash. In the list, select
  several to change their status or favourite them together.

## Creating a project

Choose **New Project**, or **New project** in the title bar's **+** menu. The dialog takes a
**Name**, a description, a status, start and due dates, a colour, and a list of tasks to create in
it. **Create Project** opens the new project.

You can also turn a task into a project: **Convert to project** in the task's **…** menu. Its
subtasks become the project's tasks.

## Status and progress

A project's status is one of **Not started**, **Active**, **On hold** or **Completed**. You set it;
DevBrain doesn't change it for you. Marking it Completed records the day, which then shows as "Done
Nov 12".

Progress is worked out from its tasks: the completed ones out of all of them. Cancelled tasks and
tasks in the trash don't count, and a project with no tasks is at 0%.

## A project's page

  page="project"
  alt="A project's Overview: name, status, dates and time left, description, open tasks, and a side panel with progress by status and the project's notes"
  focusX={0.2}
/>

The page has three tabs.

### Overview

The project as a document: its name, status, **start → due** dates with the time left ("12 days
left", "2 days overdue"), what mentions it ("2 mentions", see
[Mentioned in](/docs/notes/#mentioned-in)), and its description. Below come its **Open tasks**, with a row to add one,
and its **Recently completed** tasks.

On the right, **Progress** shows the percentage and how many tasks are Completed, In progress, Not
started and overdue; click a line to see those tasks. **Notes** lists the project's latest notes,
with a **+** for a new one.

Click the coloured folder to change the project's colour. A new, empty project has a **Move Tasks
Here** button, which shows your tasks that aren't in any project.

### Tasks

The project's tasks, with everything the [Tasks page](/docs/tasks/#the-tasks-page) has: list,
grouped and board views, filters and sorting. It's grouped by status at first, and new tasks go in
this project.

### Notes

The project's notes as cards, most recently edited first, with **New Note**.

### The project's … menu

**New task** and **New note** (already in this project), **Change colour**, **Copy as Markdown**
(its details, description and tasks as a checklist) and **Move to trash**.

## Moving things into a project

- A task: its **Project** property, or **Move** with several selected.
- A note: **+ Add to project** under its title.
- From [Home](/docs/home/#quick-capture): type `#Project name` in the capture box.

## Deleting a project

**Move to trash** takes the project out of your lists, with its tasks and notes. Restoring it from
**Trash** brings them back with it. A task or note you'd moved to the trash on its own before stays
there; restore it separately. When you delete the project for good, its tasks and notes are kept,
without a project.

## Projects from Linear

The Linear projects of the issues assigned to you come in as projects too. Like their issues,
they're read-only in DevBrain: their status, dates, colour and description come from Linear.
