you choose, side by side, and ties each meeting to its notes and follow-up tasks.

## Views and moving around

- **Month**, **Week** and **Day**. Month shows up to three events a day, then "+2 more"; click a
  date or "+2 more" to open that day. Week and day show an **All day** row, the hours, and a red
  line at the current time.
- **Today** comes back to today; the arrows go to the previous or next month, week or day.
- In a wide window, a side panel shows a small month to jump to any day; today's meeting **Up
  next** (or **Now** while it runs), with how soon it starts and a **Join** button; and an agenda of
  **Today** and **Tomorrow**: their events, and the tasks due on them.

The calendar opens in the **Default view** from [Settings › Calendar](/docs/settings/#calendar),
where you can also choose the first day of the week and **Hide weekends**. Times are shown in your
computer's time zone, in the 12- or 24-hour **Time format** you choose.

Each event is tinted with its calendar's colour, and overlapping events are stacked so every title
stays readable. How an event looks tells you more: one you've declined is faded, one you answered "maybe" (or
that's tentative) has a dashed edge, and one with a meeting link has a camera icon.

## Which calendars show

**Filter** lists your calendars: your local ones, and each connected Google account's. Untick a
calendar to hide its events; **Show all** brings them all back. DevBrain remembers what you hid.

Events you create go in a **local calendar**, which lives only in your workspace. Google calendars
are chosen in [Integrations](/docs/integrations/google-calendar/#choosing-calendars).

## Creating an event

- Click a time in the week or day view, or drag across several, to start an event there.
- Click the **All day** row or a day in the month view for an all-day event.
- Choose **New Event**, or **New event** in the title bar's **+** menu.
- Type it on [Home](/docs/home/#events): `Design review tomorrow at 3pm`.

The **New event** dialog has a title, **When** (the day, start and end times, and an **All day**
switch), **Repeat**, **Calendar**, **Location**, **Meeting link** and **Description**. To create
it, choose **Create Event** or press <kbd>⌘ Enter</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl Enter</kbd>).

New events last the **Default event length** at first. Events can't be dragged to another time
yet: change their time in the event's sheet.

### Repeating events

**Repeat** can be **Every day**, **Every weekday**, **Every week** (on the event's weekday),
**Every month** or **Every year**. A repeating event stays at the same local time when the clocks
change.

When you change or delete one occurrence of a repeating event, DevBrain asks which events to
change:

- **This event**: only this one.
- **This and following events**: this one and every later one.
- **All events**: the whole series.

## An event's sheet

Click an event to open it in a sheet beside the calendar.

For your own events, everything in the sheet can be edited where it is: the title, **Date**,
**Time**, **Repeats**, **Calendar**, **Location**, **Meeting** link (with a **Join** button) and the
description. **Delete event** removes it; its note and linked tasks are kept.

Below, every event, including Google's, has:

- **Event note**: the meeting's note (see below).
- **Linked tasks**: tasks linked to this event. **Add** creates a task already linked to it.

### Meeting notes

Open an event's note to take notes during the meeting. With no note yet, **Start Note** creates one
named after the event ("Standup notes") (or press <kbd>N</kbd>), and **Link Existing Note** links one you already
have. The note opens in the sheet, under a strip with the event's time, place and **Join** button,
so you can join the call and write at once. **Open as page** opens it in the full editor.

The note stays linked to the event: it appears in the [Notes](/docs/notes/) page with the event's
pill, and the event shows its note.

## Google Calendar events

Events from Google Calendar are read-only in DevBrain, with a **Google Calendar** badge. Their sheet
shows your **Response** (Going, Maybe, Not going or Not answered); change them, or your response,
in Google Calendar (**Open in Google Calendar**). You can still give them a note and linked tasks.

See [Integrations](/docs/integrations/google-calendar/) for which events sync.
