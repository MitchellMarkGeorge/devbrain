## Your first workspace

The first time DevBrain opens, it shows **Welcome to DevBrain**. Everything you make in DevBrain
lives in a [workspace](/docs/workspaces/), so start by making one:

1. Choose **Create Workspace**.
2. Give it a **Workspace name** (for example "Personal" or your company's name), and pick a
   **Colour** for its tile.
3. Choose **Create Workspace**. DevBrain opens it on [Home](/docs/home/).

If you already have a workspace from another computer, choose **Open Existing Folder…** instead
(see [moving a workspace](/docs/workspaces/#moving-a-workspace-to-another-computer)).

Next time, the Welcome screen lists your **Recent workspaces**, but you'll rarely see it: DevBrain
reopens the last workspace you used, on the page you left it on.

:::tip
You can keep several workspaces, say one for your job and one for side projects. They don't share
anything, and you switch between them from the workspace menu.
:::

### The sidebar

- **Home**, **Tasks**, **Projects**, **Notes** and **Calendar**: the workspace's pages.
- **Recents**: the last five tasks, notes and projects you opened.
- **Favorites**: what you've starred. Star a task, note or project with the star in the title bar,
  or press <kbd>F</kbd> in a list. **More** shows the rest when there are more than five.
- At the bottom, the **sync status** ("Synced 2 min ago", "Set up sync", …), which opens
  [Integrations](/docs/integrations/), then buttons for **Trash**, **Settings** and **Help** (the
  [keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/)).

Click **Recents** or **Favorites** to fold them away. Press <kbd>⌘\\</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl \\</kbd>) or
use the sidebar button in the title bar to hide the sidebar; rest the pointer on the window's left
edge to peek at it while it's hidden. In a narrow window (under about 1080 pixels) the sidebar
becomes a drawer that slides over the page.

### The title bar

From left to right:

- **The workspace menu**, showing the workspace's name and colour. It lists your workspaces (click
  one to switch to it) and has **New workspace…**, **Open folder…** and **Show in Finder** (Explorer on Windows, File Manager on Linux), which
  opens the workspace's folder in your file manager.
- **Back** and **Forward**, like a browser's.
- **The search field.** It shows the title of the page you're on; click it, or press <kbd>⌘K</kbd>
  (<kbd>Ctrl K</kbd>), to [search](/docs/search/).
- **The + menu**: **New task**, **New note**, **New project** and **New event**.
- **The page's actions**, on the right. A task, note or project has a star (**Add to favorites**)
  and a **…** menu with what you can do to it: duplicate it, copy it as Markdown, move it to the
  trash, and more.

On Windows, the **Menu** button at the top left opens the app's menu. On macOS and Linux the menu
is in the menu bar.

### Pages and sheets

Most things open as a page: a task, a note or a project fills the window, and **Back** returns you
to the list. A few open in a **sheet** beside the page instead, so you keep your place: a task you
click on the board, an event in the calendar, and a note opened from either. A sheet's button opens
it as a full page.
