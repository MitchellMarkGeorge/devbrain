---
title: Integrations
description: Bring your Linear issues and Google Calendar events into DevBrain.
---

DevBrain can mirror **Linear** and **Google Calendar** into a workspace, so the work you track
elsewhere sits in the same lists, boards and calendar as your own. Connect them in
**Settings › Integrations**. Each workspace has its own connections.

Mirrored items are **read-only** in DevBrain, and nothing is sent back: change them in Linear or
Google Calendar. You can still link them to your own notes, tasks and events, and each shows where
it came from.

## Linear

Connect Linear with a personal API key, which you create in Linear under **Settings › Security &
access**. DevBrain brings in the issues assigned to you, with their projects and sub-issues.

## Google Calendar

Sign in with Google, then choose which calendars to show. Their events appear in DevBrain's
calendar beside your own.

## Syncing

DevBrain checks for changes every five minutes, when you come back to its window, when a workspace
opens, and when you ask it to. There's no DevBrain server in between: the app talks to Linear and
Google directly.

Your sign-ins are encrypted with your computer's keychain and never leave the app.

## Disconnecting

To stop syncing for a while, disable the integration in Settings. To disconnect it, choose what
happens to what it brought in:

- **Keep** it: the items stay in DevBrain as your own, stop syncing and become editable.
- **Remove** it: the items are deleted, except those a note or task links to, which stay as local
  copies.
