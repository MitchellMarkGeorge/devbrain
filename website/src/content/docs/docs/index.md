DevBrain is a desktop app for planning a developer's work. Your **tasks**, **projects**, **notes**
and **calendar** live in one window, linked to each other. Your **Linear** issues and **Google
Calendar** events appear beside them.

## How it works

- **Everything is on your computer.** A [workspace](/docs/workspaces/) is a folder on your disk. It
  holds a database for your tasks, projects and events, and a Markdown file for each note.
- **No account, no server.** There is no DevBrain account or cloud, and no telemetry. DevBrain only
  goes online to sync the [integrations](/docs/integrations/) you connect, and it talks to Linear and
  Google directly.
- **Sync brings things in; it doesn't send them back.** Linear issues and Google events are mirrored
  into DevBrain and stay read-only there. You change them in Linear or Google Calendar.
- **Your notes are plain Markdown.** You can read them in any editor, and copy, import or export
  them as `.md` files.

[Data and privacy](/docs/data-and-privacy/) explains this in more detail.

## Start here

1. [Install DevBrain](/docs/install/) on macOS, Windows or Linux.
2. [Create your first workspace](/docs/getting-started/) and take the tour of the window.
3. [Connect Linear or Google Calendar](/docs/integrations/), or
   [import the tasks and notes](/docs/import-export/) you already have.

## What's in a workspace

| Area                        | What it holds                                                                |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Home](/docs/home/)         | Today at a glance, and a box to capture a task, note or event in one line    |
| [Tasks](/docs/tasks/)       | Lists, groups and boards, with statuses, priorities, dates and subtasks      |
| [Projects](/docs/projects/) | Tasks and notes grouped toward a goal, with dates and progress               |
| [Notes](/docs/notes/)       | Rich text that reads and writes Markdown, saved as files                     |
| [Calendar](/docs/calendar/) | Your events by month, week or day, including the Google calendars you choose |
| [Search](/docs/search/)     | <kbd>⌘K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl K</kbd> on Windows and Linux) searches all of it    |
