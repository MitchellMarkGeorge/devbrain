DevBrain is a desktop app for planning a developer's work: **tasks**, **projects**, **notes** and
your **calendar** in one place, with your **Linear** issues and **Google Calendar** events brought
in beside them.

It's local first. Everything you make is stored on your computer, in a
[workspace](/docs/workspaces/) folder you own. There's no DevBrain account and no server.

## Start here

- [Install DevBrain](/docs/install/) on macOS, Windows or Linux.
- Create your first workspace when DevBrain opens. You can make more later, one per job or side
  project, and switch between them from the workspace menu.
- [Connect Linear or Google Calendar](/docs/integrations/), or
  [import tasks and notes](/docs/import-export/) you already have.

## What's in a workspace

| Area     | What it holds                                                                    |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tasks    | Lists and boards grouped by status, with priorities, dates and subtasks          |
| Projects | An overview, the project's tasks and its notes, with its health and progress     |
| Notes    | Rich text that reads and writes Markdown, saved as files                         |
| Calendar | Your events on a time grid, including the Google calendars you choose            |
| Search   | Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl K</kbd> on Windows and Linux) to search all of it |
